# Business Model — Overview

> Phase 1 — Strategy

Business model work defines how a product creates, delivers, and captures value. It bridges company strategy with product decisions, ensuring that what you build is commercially viable and competitively defensible. Key outputs include canvases, value proposition designs, and business case artefacts.
