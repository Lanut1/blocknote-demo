# Communication — Overview

> Phase 5 — People

Communication work covers the PM practices that keep stakeholders aligned, decisions visible, and teams coordinated. It produces the artefacts that replace ad hoc updates with structured, repeatable communication rhythms — from weekly status reports to decision logs that capture institutional knowledge.
