# Agile & Process — Overview

> Phase 4 — Delivery

Agile and process work establishes the operating rhythm, ceremonies, and agreements that enable a product team to ship consistently and improve continuously. It covers sprint planning, retrospectives, and the quality standards that prevent ambiguity from slowing delivery.
