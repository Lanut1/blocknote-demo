# Lean & Experiments — Overview

> Phase 4 — Delivery

Lean and experiments work applies the Build-Measure-Learn discipline to reduce the risk of building the wrong thing. It covers hypothesis formation, experiment design, and the structured logging that builds institutional knowledge about what works and what doesn't.
