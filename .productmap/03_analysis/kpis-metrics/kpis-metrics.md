# KPIs & Metrics — Overview

> Phase 3 — Analysis

KPI and metrics work establishes the measurement system that tells product teams whether their bets are working. It spans from defining the north star metric down to input metrics, ensuring every team member knows what success looks like and how their work connects to business outcomes.
